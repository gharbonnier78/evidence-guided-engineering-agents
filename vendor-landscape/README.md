# Vendor landscape assessment

## Purpose

Evaluate commercial components without confusing a feature demonstration with an end-to-end governed capability.

## Capability layers

1. Enterprise retrieval and permission-aware connectors.
2. Invention capture and disclosure management.
3. Patent and non-patent prior-art search.
4. Claim mapping, FTO, prosecution, and portfolio workflows.
5. Legal and regulatory research.
6. Funding discovery and financial modeling.
7. BOM, sizing, SBOM, IaC, and operational feedback.
8. Workflow orchestration, human approval, and audit trail.

## Assessment questions

- Does the product inspect submitted materials or discover candidates proactively?
- Which source systems and permission models are supported?
- Are citations evidence-linked and exportable?
- How are uncertainty, disagreement, and missing evidence represented?
- Can models, prompts, rules, and source versions be replayed?
- What data is retained, where, for how long, and for model training?
- Can external transmission be blocked until named human approval?
- Does the vendor separate patentability, FTO, ownership, and business value?
- Can the component be removed or replaced without losing the decision record?
- What measurable improvement over the current workflow has been demonstrated?

## Initial market hypothesis

Commercial products cover many individual layers. The full workflow—ambient but authorized discovery, preliminary falsification, uncertainty-aware opportunity analysis, expert matching, consent-controlled transmission, and outcome learning—should be treated as a composed architecture until verified otherwise.
