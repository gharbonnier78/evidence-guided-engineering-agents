# Existing context — market, acquisition, transition and capability ecosystem

**Status:** working baseline to preserve prior decisions before Market Discovery v0.

## Purpose

This note records the already-established business, acquisition, transition, and capability-market ideas that pre-date Market Discovery v0. It is intentionally a traceability document, not a new business plan. Where an item is still an assumption, it is marked as such rather than being promoted to fact.

## 1. Existing repository foundation

The parent repository already defines an evidence-guided decision chain:

`authorized source → signal → claim/assumption → challenge/falsification → evidence → uncertainty → human decision → bounded action → observed outcome/replay`.

The repository also already contains a `Funding & Business Evidence` persona family with roles for opportunity scouting, investment/business-case modelling, assumption red-teaming and evidence planning. The governing rule remains: a role or tool is justified only when it changes a real decision or the confidence behind it.

## 2. Existing entrepreneurial trajectory

The long-horizon direction already discussed is not to begin by building a large marketplace or autonomous-agent platform. The intended progression is evidence-led and financed progressively by real use:

1. deliver high-value complex-systems / engineering work personally and with bounded AI assistance;
2. identify repeated problem classes and reusable methods;
3. externalize them into explicit capabilities, evidence contracts, playbooks and tools;
4. bring in named human specialists only when a real mission requires them;
5. progressively add connectors, retrieval, calculation, simulation, drafting and orchestration assistance;
6. only after repeated demand, expose composable human+AI capabilities through a marketplace/orchestration layer;
7. preserve accountable human authority for consequential, regulated or expert decisions.

A previously discussed target framing is an **AI-Augmented Complex Systems Architect / Architecte de systèmes complexes et d’ingénierie augmentée par IA**, with ML, optimisation, simulation, operations research, control, hybrid/quantum techniques and other methods treated as tools selected because the problem warrants them, not as marketing labels in search of a use case.

A prior managed-service formulation was also identified: a Human–AI decision service integrating client engineering environments, operational evidence, AI agents and on-demand human expertise under explicit risk and budget governance.

## 3. Human expert marketplace / capability composition already discussed

The intended future marketplace is not a catalogue of anonymous chatbot personas. The existing idea is capability composition such as:

`Need → Capability → Evidence required → Human expert → AI assistance / connectors / tools → Deliverable → Human authority`.

For inventions/IP work, a concrete earlier chain was:

`authorized-work harvest → candidate technical contribution → prior-art/falsification → related ideas → protection options → human gate → local IP referent or qualified expert/cabinet`.

Potential expert matching factors already discussed include technical coverage, geography, independence/conflicts, cost, timing and experience. Automatic delegation is explicitly out of scope for consequential expert authority.

## 4. Existing company acquisition / turnaround / succession watch

A France-wide watch was defined for B2B SMEs with the following current target envelope:

- **Revenue:** €2M–€15M;
- **Employees:** 15–120;
- **Sectors:** software / ESN, engineering, testing and quality, cybersecurity, digital identity and biometrics, vision and electronics, inspection and certification, and systems integration.

Existing source families include Actify, BODACC, transmission platforms and public company sources.

Existing target filters:

- notify only a **new individual target** with attractiveness **≥65/100** and risk **≤60/100**;
- notify only a **cluster / consolidation thesis** with consolidation potential **≥70/100** and risk **≤60/100**;
- always include sources, available financials, deadline when applicable, important unknowns and a recommended next action;
- do not notify when nothing clears the evidence threshold.

For combinations of companies, the already-defined analysis must cover:

- complementary vs redundant activities;
- geographic compatibility;
- cross-selling and customer overlap;
- shared management and support functions;
- tool, certification and technical-capability mutualisation;
- cumulative cash needs;
- social and integration risks;
- recommended acquisition order;
- structural option: holding with subsidiaries, asset acquisition followed by integration, or later merger.

The strategic motivation is not simply financial acquisition. A target can also be interesting because it carries scarce engineering capabilities, reusable customer access, certifications, delivery machinery or expert talent that could strengthen the future capability ecosystem.

## 5. Market discovery direction already established

The prospecting objective is not to maximize lead volume. The agreed direction is to find organizations with observable, expensive engineering problems for which a bounded first intervention is credible.

Candidate observable problem signals already discussed include:

- complex RFPs and contractual requirements;
- verification, validation, qualification, certification and assurance obligations;
- complex or regulated systems;
- systems / system-of-systems engineering;
- AI/ML industrialisation or assurance needs;
- test-environment generation and provisioning;
- DevSecOps, CI/CD and Infrastructure as Code;
- simulation and digital twins;
- heterogeneous system integration;
- traceability from requirements to architecture to tests to evidence;
- supply-chain / supplier qualification;
- scarce expertise needs;
- optimisation, operations research and decision under uncertainty where justified;
- biometrics, identity and cybersecurity where a concrete problem exists.

The first commercial unit should be a **problem-solving outcome**, not access to agents or tokens. Candidate early offers discussed include a bounded diagnostic, an evidence-guided engineering sprint, or a mission around a concrete architecture / qualification / RFP / AI-assurance decision.

## 6. Economic discipline already agreed

No paid database, premium AI plan, connector, hosted platform or marketplace infrastructure should be treated as a prerequisite. The working economic rule is:

`contribution margin = client price − external human expertise − paid data − compute/AI − other variable costs`

and then:

`operating result = Σ contribution margins − fixed costs`.

A paid tool is promoted only when its decision value or time saving is evidenced against its cost. The intended sequencing is therefore: prove willingness-to-pay and repeatable value first; automate and subscribe second.

The repository's existing `Funding & Business Evidence` logic applies directly: start with the smallest reversible tranche, state the evidence expected, and predefine **continue / revise / scale / stop** criteria.

## 7. Initial funnel hypothesis to test, not assume

A deliberately small initial funnel was discussed as a more useful target than mass outbound prospecting:

`qualified organizations → discovery conversations → concrete problem statements → proposals → paid pilot(s) → measured value / margin / repeat demand`.

An indicative early milestone previously discussed was roughly:

`20 qualified problems → 10 interviews → 3 proposals → 1 paid pilot`.

These numbers are **starting hypotheses**, not success criteria frozen in advance. Market Discovery v0 must measure and revise them.

## 8. Classification model to preserve

An organization may play more than one role. Do not force a mutually exclusive label. Current useful classifications are:

- acquisition / turnaround target;
- consolidation partner;
- potential client;
- design partner;
- human expert / capability provider;
- technology or data provider;
- channel / ecosystem partner;
- research / laboratory partner.

The same company can legitimately occupy several categories when evidence supports it.

## 9. Non-goals carried forward

Market Discovery v0 must not silently become any of the following:

- mass lead scraping;
- a generic AI-consulting positioning exercise;
- a marketplace build before repeated demand exists;
- autonomous consequential decision-making;
- a justification for buying expensive data before its marginal value is demonstrated;
- a technology-first quantum/AI/ML sales pitch disconnected from a client problem;
- a static business plan optimized to persuade rather than a falsifiable model updated by evidence.

## 10. Relationship to Market Discovery v0

This file is the baseline. Market Discovery v0 begins by converting this accumulated context into an explicit, testable market-learning protocol: sources, observable signals, target selection, interview method, offer hypotheses, economic measures, evidence records, decision gates and replay.
