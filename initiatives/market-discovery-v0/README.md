# Market Discovery v0

Market Discovery v0 is the evidence-guided market-learning initiative for the `evidence-guided-engineering-agents` repository.

It connects three previously separate but related questions:

1. **What complex engineering problems are organizations demonstrably facing?**
2. **Which capabilities can solve enough of those problems to justify paid work and progressive automation?**
3. **Which companies or experts are interesting not only as clients, but also as acquisition/transition targets, consolidation partners, capability providers or future ecosystem participants?**

## Current artifacts

- [`EXISTING_CONTEXT.md`](EXISTING_CONTEXT.md) — trace of prior decisions, acquisition/turnaround criteria, long-horizon human+AI capability vision, economic discipline and market-discovery assumptions.
- [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md) — Market Discovery v0 experiment plan, claims, falsifiers, sources, funnel, economics, gates and first execution sequence.

## Governing principle

This initiative does not assume that the future marketplace, AI personas, connectors, premium data or automation should exist. It must earn those layers through evidence from real problems and real economic behavior.

The immediate objective is therefore deliberately small:

`public evidence → qualified problem → conversation → bounded proposal → paid pilot → measured value/economics → revised prior`

Acquisition/transition analysis and market-demand analysis may share evidence, but they remain separate decisions and must not be conflated.
