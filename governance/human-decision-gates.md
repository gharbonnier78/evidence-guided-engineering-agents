# Human decision gates

## Action classes

| Class | Examples | Minimum gate |
|---|---|---|
| Observe | Retrieve, compare, summarize authorized material | Logged operation |
| Recommend | Rank options, draft a BOM, propose an experiment | Named reviewer |
| Prepare | Create IaC, disclosure, business case, or external message draft | Domain expert review |
| Transmit | Send data or a dossier outside the approved domain | Explicit approver and exact-recipient confirmation |
| Commit | File, contract, fund, deploy, release, or change production | Existing formal authority; agent cannot self-authorize |

## Decision record

The human decision must capture:

- accepted, rejected, deferred, or conditionally accepted;
- evidence considered;
- known residual uncertainty;
- dissent;
- conditions and expiry;
- owner of follow-up action;
- outcome signal expected.

No-response is not approval.
