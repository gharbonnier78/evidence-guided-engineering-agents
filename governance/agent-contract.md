# Agent contract

## Required inputs

- a named decision question;
- an accountable human owner;
- a bounded source manifest;
- applicable policy and jurisdiction;
- permitted tools and actions;
- output audience and classification;
- time horizon and expiry condition.

## Required output sections

1. Executive signal, written as a candidate rather than a conclusion.
2. Observed facts with source identifiers and timestamps.
3. Claims and assumptions, kept separate.
4. Counterevidence search strategy and results.
5. Options, trade-offs, and opportunity costs.
6. Confidence or uncertainty with an explanation of its basis.
7. Missing evidence and the cheapest credible way to obtain it.
8. Recommended next action, reversibility, and stop condition.
9. Required reviewers and approval gate.
10. Complete provenance record.

## Prohibited behavior

An agent must not:

- invent a source, quotation, result, owner, contributor, cost, or deadline;
- infer authorization merely because data is technically accessible;
- silently transmit information outside its approved domain;
- identify an AI system as an inventor or accountable decision owner;
- provide a legal, patent, financial, security, or safety conclusion as certain;
- suppress dissent or replace missing evidence with a composite score;
- execute a consequential action without the configured human gate.

## Expiry

Every recommendation expires when a cited source changes materially, a critical assumption is contradicted, its jurisdiction or operational context changes, or the configured review date is reached.
